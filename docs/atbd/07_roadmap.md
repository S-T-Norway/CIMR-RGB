# Roadmap

This version of the ATBD describes the CIMR RGB as released. The further development of the toolbox will be carried out in a
Contract Change Notice (CCN) for continued development, expected to start in Q4 2026.

The scope of this development builds on:

- the gap analysis and implementation strategies for the evolution of the RGB towards the needs of the CIMR Level-2 processors,
  described in the ATBD for RGB evolution {doc}`[RD-6]<03_references>`;
- the developments needed for the CIMR L1C processor, listed in the roadmap of the CIMR L1C ATBD
  ({ref}`CIMR L1C ATBD <l1c:sec-rm>` {doc}`[RD-5]<03_references>`).

This document will be updated as these developments are implemented.
